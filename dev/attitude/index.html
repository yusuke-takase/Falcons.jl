Time-dependent rotations and external attitude

Any rotation of the chain q(t) = A(t) · S(t) · bore2spin · d(t) (see Conventions) except bore2spin can be a time series. Time series are RotQuaternions: quaternions sampled at the absolute times start_time_s + k / sampling_rate_hz and interpolated with slerp.

using Falcons

q0 = detector_quat(deg2rad(2), deg2rad(120))
t = 0.0:1.0:4000.0
wobble = [quat_rotation_x(deg2rad(1 / 60) * sin(2π * tk / 97)) * q0 for tk in t]
d = RotQuaternion(wobble; start_time_s = 0.0, sampling_rate_hz = 1.0)

A series must cover every time at which it is evaluated: otherwise the computation stops with an ArgumentError (litebird_sim silently wraps around instead).

Detector quaternions

Pass a time series as DetectorInfo(quat = d). In the IMO the same information is a dictionary with start_time (seconds), sampling_rate_hz and quats (see Instrument database and parameter files).

External attitude

The first argument of an Observation is the attitude A(t), the rotation from the spin-axis frame to Ecliptic coordinates. Instead of a SpinningScanningStrategy it can be a time series, for instance a measured or simulated attitude:

# quats::Vector{Quaternion{Float64}}: spin-axis frame → Ecliptic, sampled every second from t0
attitude = RotQuaternion(quats; start_time_s = t0, sampling_rate_hz = 1.0)
obs = Observation(attitude, inst, dets; start_time_s = t0, duration_s = 3600.0)

Falcons does not read attitude files: convert the data to this frame before building the RotQuaternion. Constant frame changes can be applied to the whole series with a product, e.g. inertial_to_ecl * RotQuaternion(quats; ...) for a rotation on the left (from the data's inertial frame to Ecliptic) or RotQuaternion(quats; ...) * spin_to_body on the right (from the spin-axis frame to the data's body frame). The angles of the InstrumentInfo are then defined with respect to that spin-axis frame.

Perturbations of the nominal attitude

To keep the analytic scan and add a small rotation in the spin-axis frame (for example a pointing error or a wobble), pass it as spin_perturbation:

obs = Observation(scan, inst, dets; start_time_s = 0.0, duration_s = 3600.0,
                  spin_perturbation = RotQuaternion(dq; start_time_s = 0.0, sampling_rate_hz = 10.0))

This is the position where litebird_sim's SpacecraftCoord adds its disturbances.

Exact and interpolated pointings

By default every sample is computed exactly: the analytic attitude is evaluated at the sample time and the time series are interpolated to it. With Observation(...; delta_time_s = Δ) Falcons reproduces litebirdsim instead: the boresight rotation A · S · bore2spin is computed on anchors every Δ seconds (counted from `starttimesimulations`) and interpolated, and time-dependent detector quaternions are interpolated separately. The anchors are computed for one block of samples at a time, so their memory does not grow with the length of the observation. Both modes are available on the CPU and on the GPU.
