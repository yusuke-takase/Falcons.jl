Conventions

Falcons uses the conventions of litebird_sim. The test suite compares Falcons with litebird_sim on reference configurations (test/reference/), so the statements below are checked numerically.

Quaternions

Quaternions are stored as (x, y, z, w), products follow the Hamilton convention and rotations are active: a vector v is rotated to q v q* (rotate_vector). q1 * q2 applies q2 first. quat_rotation(θ, axis) and quat_rotation_x/y/z(θ) build right-handed rotations by θ radians.

Frames and the rotation chain

  • Detector frame: the beam points along +z, the polarisation reference along +x.
  • Boresight frame: a detector is placed with its quaternion d (detector → boresight), see detector_quat.
  • Spin-axis frame: the boresight frame is rotated by bore2spin = Rz(spin_rotangle) · Ry(spin_boresight_angle) · Rz(boresight_rotangle) (InstrumentInfo).
  • Ecliptic frame: the spin-axis frame is rotated by the attitude A(t) = Rz(Ω t) · Rx(2π f_prec t) · Ry(π/2 − α) · Rz(2π f_spin t) (SpinningScanningStrategy, spin_to_ecliptic), with α the spin–anti-Sun angle and Ω = 2π / (365.25 days). At t = 0 the anti-Sun direction is +x and the Ecliptic north pole is +z.

The quaternion of a detector at the time t is therefore

q(t) = A(t) · S(t) · bore2spin · d(t)

where S(t) is an optional perturbation in the spin-axis frame and A, S and d can also be time series (see Time-dependent rotations and external attitude). For Galactic outputs the Ecliptic-to- Galactic rotation is multiplied on the left; its values are litebird_sim's (astropy, BarycentricMeanEcliptic → galactic).

Times are absolute. An Observation samples t_k = start_time_s + k / sampling_rate_hz, and the spin, precession and anti-Sun phases are evaluated at t_k itself, as litebird_sim does for a floating-point start time. Calendar dates and ephemerides are not supported.

Pointing angles

For every sample get_pointings returns (θ, φ, ψ):

  • θ ∈ [0, π]: colatitude of the beam direction q ẑ q*;
  • φ ∈ [0, 2π): its longitude;
  • ψ ∈ (−π, π]: orientation of q x̂ q*, measured from the local East (e_φ) towards the local North (−e_θ).

The detector polarisation angle (DetectorInfo.pol_angle_rad) is not included in ψ.

Half-wave plate

An IdealHWP has the mechanical angle

χ(t) = start_angle_rad + ang_speed_radpsec · (t − start_time_simulation_s)

wrapped to [−π, π]. It is measured from the start of the simulation, so observations split with split_observation keep a continuous phase. The angle is evaluated in closed form, while litebird_sim accumulates it with a recurrence; the two differ by rounding only (about 1e-12 rad after ten minutes at 19 Hz).

In litebirdsim's signal model the polarisation angle seen by a detector behind an ideal HWP is `2χ − polangle_rad + ψ`.

h-maps

make_h_maps computes, in every pixel p and for integer spin orders (n, m),

h_{n,m}(p) = (1 / N_p) Σ_{samples in p} exp(+i (n ψ + m χ))

with N_p the number of hits, as make_h_maps in litebirdsim. For (n, m) = (0, 0) the real part holds `Npinstead, and pixels without hits are 0. Observations of the same detector (matched by name) are accumulated before normalising. Files written by [savehmaps](@ref) use litebird_sim's layout (hmapsdet_<name>.h5, one group"n,m"withReandIm` per couple).

Pixelisation

Maps use HEALPix with 1-based pixel indices (Healpix.jl's convention; subtract 1 for healpy). Hit maps, pixel indices and h-maps default to Galactic coordinates and RING ordering, as in litebird_sim; pointings default to Ecliptic coordinates.
